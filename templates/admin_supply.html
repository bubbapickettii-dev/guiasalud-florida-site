Lead supply — researched sources & agreements

THE HARD RULE (evidenced in the registry): buying a lead is NOT permission to resell or redistribute it. At least one reviewed publisher agreement forbids resale/transfer of a lead's consumer information unless the insertion order expressly permits it. Before ANY external supply flows into this marketplace: sign the vendor/publisher agreement, get named downstream recipients + channels + per-lead buyer caps in writing, require the consent certificate (TrustedForm/Jornaya) to accompany each lead, and record the agreement reference in Agents > Buyers verification. Until then every row below stays supply-DISABLED: no consumer data may enter from it.

A consumer lead with no assignable buyer is still NEVER relieved of that rule: routing refuses it (NO_BUYER / NOT_ROUTABLE) and it is never "sold free" to a third party. {{ d.note }}
