Signal watch — public intent signals (review queue)

Signals are not leads. Each row is a public post where someone openly describes a coverage problem ("lost my job", "my plan was cancelled", "me cambiaron de seguro"). The author is never identified or contacted: no usernames, no message fields, no DMs, no replies. The product of a review is a decision about a public asset — which content page answers that moment, or which channel/test the finding should join — never an outreach task. The consumer side of this business still lives entirely in the Check: someone submits their own phone there, with recorded consent, before anything is routed.

Statuses: NEW (needs a decision) · REVIEWED (read, awaiting the evidence note) · ACTIONED (decision recorded with evidence) · IRRELEVANT. Fetching is manual: python tools/signals_watch.py fetch --network (Reddit/Hacker News public endpoints, never automatic) or offline --ingest file.json.
