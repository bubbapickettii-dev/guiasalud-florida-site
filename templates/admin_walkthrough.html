2 · AI & data collection

Public-web signals → local AI. Collection is manual only (a CLI action you run); nothing sweeps on a schedule. Public posts are stored minimized (no author handles); the local Ollama model classifies and distills them.

Signal watchReview queue of public posts (Reddit/HN/YouTube/News/webreader) classified EN/ES with relevance scores. Signals are never leads and never contact targets.Filter by language/topic; open a citation URL; mark one REVIEWED with a note

Supply sourcesResearched external lead-supply registry (all supply-disabled by default)Read — this page sells nothing

CLI (run in the project folder, e.g. .venv\Scripts\python.exe -X utf8 tools\collect.py …): status (what's stored) · run --network (explicit sweep, overlap-locked, politely pauses) · clusters-build (local-AI distillation) · ai-health / ai-smoke (honest AI checks) · report (markdown report to out/). Collection stays off unless you execute it.
