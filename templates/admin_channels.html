Two different jobs. Demand discovery (scrapers, signal watch, public registries) finds people who are already asking for help and feeds the prospect ledger — it never contacts them. Demand capture (the links above, guides, partner pages, the free-help directory) brings those people to this site to run the check themselves. Only a capture visit can become a consented inquiry.
