Losing job-based health coverage opens a Special Enrollment Period: you usually have 60 days from the day your coverage ends to enroll in a Marketplace plan.

{% if e.closure %}

When a company shuts down completely, its health plan usually ends too, so COBRA usually isn't available. In that case a Marketplace plan (60-day window from the day coverage ended, or Open Enrollment) is usually the main option.

{% endif %}

COBRA lets you keep the {{ e.company }} plan, but you usually pay the full cost plus up to 2%, which is often far more than you paid as an employee. You generally have 60 days to elect COBRA. If you take COBRA and later drop it on purpose, that does not open a new Special Enrollment Period, so compare first.

Help paying premiums for 2027 generally applies when household income is between $15,960 and $63,840 for one person ($33,000 to $132,000 for a family of four), under current law. Severance and unemployment benefits count as income.
