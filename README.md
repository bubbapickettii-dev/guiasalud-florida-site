# Scout (Lead Engine v3.1) — Florida health insurance lead generation

**Scout** = the system name (sent ahead to read the terrain and find the route). Discovery loop: `python tools/scout.py rank | metrics | log | report`.

**What the business is:** we generate inbound leads. A consumer completes the Health Coverage Check, types **their own phone number** and consents to one licensed agency calling them. The agent buys that lead. Public records are market intelligence only and are never contacted. Read `docs/BUSINESS_MODEL.md` first.

**Status (Oct 5, 2026): built and tested on TEST DATA only. Nothing deployed, published, paid for, or sent. No real visitors, no real leads, no channel VALIDATED.**

```
 audience ──► channel (organic / partner / paid / content) ──► /go/<experiment> or /p/<partner>
         ──► Health Coverage Check (/check, 10 angles EN+ES, branching by need)
         ──► summary ──► contact + consent naming ONE licensed agent (or email-only)
         ──► score + qualify ──► agent desk (/agent/<token>) ──► one-click statuses
         ──► Lead Quality + Distribution Lab learn which source/angle/language/partner produces sold leads ──► scale winners
```

## Consent & compliance design (built in, not bolted on)
* Checkbox unchecked by default; consent names the **platform** ("{brand} and its licensed agent partner"), never a selectable agency — consumers never choose which agent receives them. GS Allude assigns the fulfilling buyer server-side at submit (contracted criteria, daily cap, prepaid balance; onboarded buyers outrank demo placeholders).
* Consent text is re-rendered server-side from a versioned template; stored with timestamp, IP, UA, page URL and a SHA-256 of the form snapshot (snapshot HTML saved in `data/snapshots/`).
* Email-only path = NURTURE (not sold as a call lead). Health-insurance interest is captured as a flag only. A health lead needs its own consent naming a health agent.
* No buyer licensed for that state/product → the form only offers email results. A consented phone lead with no assignable buyer at submit is recorded NO_BUYER and never sold on silently.
* `/optout` flags all matching leads. Delivery is **dry-run** unless `LEADENGINE_LIVE_DELIVERY=1`.
* Status (Oct 2026): FCC one-to-one rule was vacated (11th Cir., Jan 24 2025); prior express written consent is still required for automated calls/texts (TCPA + FL s.501.059). Opt-outs must be honored within 10 business days. Have counsel review the consent copy, privacy policy and terms before launch.

## Scaling from 10 → 100+ leads/day
Infrastructure is not the bottleneck. Traffic and buyer capacity are.
1. **Math**: visits needed = leads wanted ÷ your measured visit→lead rate. There is no measured rate yet (no real traffic), so no number is assumed here; the Distribution Lab computes it per experiment once real visitors arrive. Plan volume across 4–6 channels, never one.
2. **Now (≤ ~50/day)**: one Render/VPS instance, SQLite WAL. Comfortably handles thousands of submissions a day.
3. **50–500/day**: move to Postgres. Put deliveries on a queue (RQ/Celery) with retries and buyer webhooks, add a buyer portal (accept/return, caps, pricing) and nightly CSV/API reconciliation.
4. **Quality gates buyers will demand at volume**: phone verification (OTP or carrier lookup), email verification, federal DNC + litigator scrub before delivery, a third-party consent certificate (TrustedForm/Jornaya), and return-rate tracking per campaign/source.
5. **Campaign throughput**: new campaigns are config only (`campaigns.json`). A new channel is a `/go` link plus assets. Nothing gets rebuilt.
